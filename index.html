About Us

Who Are We

Sunrise Community Hospital is a non-profit organization that is leading in medical expertise and service provision, with deservedly earned recognition throughout East Africa and beyond as an advanced diagnostic, treatment and referral centre.
For more than 70 years, Sunrise Community Hospital has been delivering quality healthcare with compassion and excellence. We are committed to improving lives through advanced medical services, experienced healthcare professionals, and patient-centered care, making a difference in our community every day.
